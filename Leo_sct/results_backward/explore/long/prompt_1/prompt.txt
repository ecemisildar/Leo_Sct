Design a supervisor for an exploration robot in a cluttered indoor space. The robot should spend most of its time exploring unseen areas, but safety has higher priority than coverage speed. If there is an object or obstacle directly in front of the robot, it must not move forward. When `obstacle_front` is detected, the controller should choose a clear recovery action such as moving backward, rotating, or performing a larger turn before allowing forward motion again. Left and right obstacles should also influence the chosen turning direction so the robot does not keep steering into blocked space. The behavior should be deterministic, avoid unsafe oscillations, and keep the robot moving whenever it is safe to do so.
