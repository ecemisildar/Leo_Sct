Generate an exploration supervisor whose goal is to safely cover the environment with reactive obstacle avoidance. Safety requirements are strict: the robot must never continue forward into `obstacle_front`, and obstacle recovery must be explicit rather than random. When obstacles are on the left or right, bias the turn choice away from the blocked side. When the path becomes clear again, the robot should resume exploration. Prefer a compact supervisor with meaningful states, stable transitions, and no ambiguous recovery choices that could re-enable unsafe forward motion too early.
