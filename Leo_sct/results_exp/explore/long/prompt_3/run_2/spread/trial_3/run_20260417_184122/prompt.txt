Generate an exploration supervisor whose goal is to safely cover the environment with reactive obstacle avoidance. Safety requirements are strict: the robot must never continue forward into `obstacle_front`, and obstacle recovery must be explicit rather than random. However, the policy should still favor coverage by spending most clear-path time in forward exploration states. When obstacles are on the left or right, bias the turn choice away from the blocked side. When the path becomes clear again, resume exploration immediately instead of adding extra scan states. Prefer a compact supervisor with meaningful states, stable transitions, and minimal idle or spin-heavy behavior.
