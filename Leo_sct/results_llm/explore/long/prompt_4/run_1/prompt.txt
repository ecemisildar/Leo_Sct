Produce a supervisor for autonomous exploration of an unknown environment. The robot should explore continuously and efficiently, with clear preference for covering space over waiting or spinning, while still prioritizing collision avoidance. Do not allow move-forward behavior when an object is detected in front of the robot. Instead, obstacle-front events should send the robot into a short safe turning or escape state, and the robot should drive forward again soon after a clear-path condition is observed. The controller should remain deterministic, should not stall, and should maintain a sensible balance between exploration and caution without getting trapped in repeated full scans.
