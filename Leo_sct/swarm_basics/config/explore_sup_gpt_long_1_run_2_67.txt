Design a supervisor for an exploration robot in a cluttered indoor space. The robot should spend most of its safe time making forward progress and covering new space, not rotating in place. If there is an object or obstacle directly in front of the robot, it must not move forward. When `obstacle_front` is detected, the controller should choose a short recovery action such as a directed turn, then return to forward exploration as soon as `path_clear` becomes true. Left and right obstacles should influence the chosen turning direction so the robot does not keep steering into blocked space. Keep the behavior deterministic, avoid unsafe oscillations, and avoid overusing `full_rotate` except as a rare last-resort recovery.
