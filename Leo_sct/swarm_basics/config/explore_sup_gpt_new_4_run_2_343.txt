Make a controller for a robot that moves around on its own and explores the area. The robot should spend most of its safe time making steady progress through the environment and covering as much new space as possible, rather than remaining in one place or repeatedly turning without progress. Its movement should include occasional small changes in direction to improve coverage, but these adjustments should remain brief and should not interrupt exploration more than necessary. When obstacles appear, the robot should react conservatively and recover with short corrective actions before continuing exploration. Obstacle avoidance should be cautious but not overly restrictive: once the way is clear again, the robot should quickly return to exploration instead of remaining in recovery behavior. Other robots may also be moving in the environment and should be treated as temporary moving obstacles that must be avoided carefully without assuming they block the path permanently.
